# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** opencircuitlab

**Short description:** Learn series, parallel, Kirchhoff's laws, nodal analysis and Thevenin equivalents, with a bridge sensor project, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** opencircuitlab is a beginner-friendly learning repo for the way electrical circuits are analysed. In three short sessions you go from bulbs in series and in parallel, to Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws, voltage dividers and the loading effect, to a general nodal-analysis solver built with numpy that you validate against hand-solved circuits and against energy conservation. You then use it for the Thevenin equivalent, maximum power transfer, and the Wheatstone bridge, including a Monte Carlo run that shows what 5 percent resistors do to a sensor reading. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All circuits are low-voltage educational models, never mains. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as nonlinear parts, amplifiers, transient circuits and a netlist reader.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
